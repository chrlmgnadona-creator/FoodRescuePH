HOW IT WORKS

Sharing food is as easy as 1, 2, 3, 4!

1️⃣

POST

Someone has extra food and lists it online.

2️⃣

FIND

Someone nearby sees the available food.

3️⃣

ASK

They send a quick request to get the food.

4️⃣

SHARE

The food is collected and shared happily!

💡 Quick Help Guide

What does “Available” mean? It means the food is still fresh and ready to be requested right now!
How do I ask for food? Click on any food card or map marker, then click the large "Ask for Food" button.
Is it safe? All providers follow strict food safety guidelines. Check out our Food Safety page for details.
