Food Safety Guidelines

Simple rules to ensure shared food is fresh, clean, and safe for everyone in the community.

🕒

1. Freshness & Timing / Sariwa at Tamang Oras

Only share food prepared within the last 24 hours. Ensure cooked meals are stored properly and packed in clean containers before pickup.

🧼

2. Clean Packaging / Malinis na Lagayan

Use sanitized food-grade containers, banana leaves, or clean resealable bags. Always wash hands thoroughly before handling food donations.

📝

3. Clear Labeling / Malinaw na Impormasyon

Clearly indicate the dish name, main ingredients (especially potential allergens like peanuts, seafood, or dairy), and the time it was cooked.
